New to CAMSAIL?

Come along to one meeting free of charge — with no obligation to join.

CAMSAIL is a friendly club for anyone interested in sailing and all things nautical. We meet monthly from October to March for illustrated talks by invited speakers and club members.

Wednesdays, 7.30 for 7.45 pm
Cambridge & County Bowling Club
Brooklands Avenue, Cambridge CB2 8FG

We also organise trips and social activities, and provide opportunities to meet other sailors. You don't need to own a boat — or even currently sail — to join us.

A little about CAMSAIL
CAMSAIL was first suggested in November 2010, when people with an interest in sailing in or near Cambridge wanted talks and the opportunity to meet others with similar interests, without having to travel to the coast.

We first met in the U3AC building in Bridge Street, then moved to the Red Bull in Newnham in October 2011. We moved to our present location, the Cambridge and County Bowling Club in Brooklands Avenue, in October 2012.
